Essential Driving Rules in Japan

Basic Rule

Keep Left

In Japan, vehicles drive on the LEFT side of the road. The driver's seat is on the right side of the car.

Crucial Sign

Japanese "STOP" Sign (止まれ)

The Japanese stop sign is an inverted red triangle with the word "止まれ" (Tomare). You MUST come to a full, complete stop before the white line.

Unique Japanese Road Markings & Signals

Road Marking

Diamond Symbol on the Road (◇)

A white diamond painted on the asphalt means "Pedestrian Crossing Ahead". Slow down and be prepared to stop. Stopping for pedestrians waiting at non-signalized crosswalks is strictly enforced by police.

Road Line

Yellow Center Line vs. White Line

A solid yellow center line strictly prohibits passing or overtaking other vehicles into the oncoming lane. Only overtake if the center line is a dashed white line and it is completely safe.

Traffic Signal

Green Arrow Signals

Even if the main traffic light is RED, you may proceed ONLY in the direction indicated by a green arrow underneath (e.g., left, right, or straight).
